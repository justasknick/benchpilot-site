Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29 · Operated by Rakessh.D

Digital service, no physical shipping. BenchPilot Pro is activated instantly on your BenchPilot account after payment.

What you receive

BenchPilot is a Chrome extension. When you pay while signed in to your BenchPilot account, Pro is switched on for that account, and on every device where you sign in. There is no key to paste and nothing to download. No physical goods are shipped and no shipping charges apply.

Delivery time

Pro activates instantly once Razorpay confirms your payment, usually within a few seconds. The Upgrade page in the extension refreshes automatically; if it does not, reopen the extension.

If Pro does not appear

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the same BenchPilot account you paid from, and reopen the extension.
  2. Wait a few minutes: bank and UPI confirmations can occasionally be delayed.
  3. If Pro has not appeared within 15 minutes, contact us at nicklennox30@gmail.com with your account email and Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). We will activate Pro or, if we cannot, reverse the charge (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).
