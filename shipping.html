Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14 · Operated by Rakessh.D

Digital service, no physical shipping. Your BenchPilot Solo plan is activated instantly on your BenchPilot account after payment.

What you receive

BenchPilot is a Chrome extension. When you pay while signed in to your BenchPilot account, the plan you bought (Solo) is switched on for that account, and on every device where you sign in. No licence key is needed for a new purchase, and there is nothing to download. Older licence keys from us still work. No physical goods are shipped and no shipping charges apply.

Team plans

When a team owner pays for a team plan (Team · 5, Team · 15 or Team · 40) in the BenchPilot web app, the months bought are added to the team's paid-until date at once, for every member of the workspace. Nothing needs to be installed again; recruiters keep using the free Chrome extension with the workspace. A team billed by invoice is switched on when the invoice payment arrives.

Delivery time

Your plan activates instantly once Razorpay confirms your payment, usually within a few seconds. The Upgrade page in the extension refreshes automatically; if it does not, reopen the extension.

If your plan does not appear

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the same BenchPilot account you paid from, and reopen the extension.
  2. Wait a few minutes: bank and UPI confirmations can occasionally be delayed.
  3. If your plan has not appeared within 15 minutes, contact us at nicklennox30@gmail.com with your account email and Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). We will activate your plan or, if we cannot, reverse the charge (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).
