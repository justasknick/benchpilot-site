Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Operated by Rakessh.D

Short version: BenchPilot is local-first. Your leads, vendor companies, consultants, resume files, notes and settings are stored in your own browser. On the Free plan they stay on your computer and are not uploaded, unless you turn on the optional account backup. On the Solo plan, encrypted sync is on by default (you can turn it off): your data is on every computer you sign in on, encrypted in your browser so only you can read it; our server holds only a locked copy it cannot read (section 2a). With sync on, your resume files are synced too, encrypted in your browser in the same way. Signed-in Free users can also choose an encrypted account backup (off until they turn it on), and if you do not sign in, nothing is uploaded. If your company uses a team workspace, the team's shared book is stored on our server so the team can work on it together; it is private to the team but it is not end-to-end encrypted (section 2b). If you create a BenchPilot account, we (and our service providers) keep your account email, sign-in details and plan and payment records. If you press Send on the feedback button, we also receive that report. If you turn on the optional "Send anonymous error reports" setting (off by default), we also receive anonymous error details when something breaks. You can delete your account yourself at any time (section 7). BenchPilot uses no AI. We do not sell your data or track you.

BenchPilot ("the extension", "we") is a Chrome extension and web app for recruiters and bench sales professionals, operated by Rakessh.D ("the seller"). This policy explains what BenchPilot handles, where it goes and who can read it. This website (the marketing site) does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

1. What data the extension reads

  • On LinkedIn pages you are viewing, BenchPilot reads the text of the posts visible on the page to find recruiter contact and requirement details: names, email addresses, phone numbers, job titles, companies, locations and required skills.
  • On other websites, the extension reads the page only when you click "Scan this page" in the extension popup. It does not read other sites in the background.
  • BenchPilot does not read your LinkedIn password, private messages or browsing history, and does not collect data from pages you have not asked it to scan. It never clicks, scrolls or messages on LinkedIn for you.
  • Consent records and contact notes you type. For a consultant you can record the date and the way they agreed to be marketed (with a short note), and for a consultant or a saved recruiter you can type short notes of calls, WhatsApp chats and meetings. BenchPilot never reads WhatsApp, your phone or other apps, and sends nothing. These stay in your browser (and in your encrypted sync copy if sync is on). "Export this consultant" gives you everything kept about one person; "Erase this consultant" deletes their profile, resume files and notes on this computer and, through sync, on your other computers, and keeps your submission counts without their name.
  • Interviews and placements you record. For a submission you can record interview times (with the time zone), the round, the panel, your notes and the outcome, and a placement: the start and end date, the bill and pay rate, tax terms and a PO number. These stay in your browser (and in your encrypted sync copy if sync is on). "Calendar" saves an .ics file on your computer for your own calendar app. "Google" and "Outlook" open your own Google Calendar or Outlook with the interview's title, time and text filled in, for you to save: your browser sends that text to Google or Microsoft only when you click. BenchPilot does not connect to any calendar.
  • Timesheets, invoices and your commission rule. For a placement you can record the hours of each week (regular and overtime), who approved them and the status, and make invoices from the approved weeks (number, period, hours, rates, amount, invoice, due and payment dates, status and a note). On Solo you can also save your own invoice details (company name, address, billing email, phone, tax ID and payment details, printed on your invoices) and your own commission rule for an estimate. These stay in your browser (and in your encrypted sync copy if sync is on). BenchPilot never sends an invoice: you print it or save it as a PDF and send it from your own email.

1a. LinkedIn profile card

The LinkedIn profile card reads only the profile page you are viewing at that moment. For your 1st-degree connections, when you open LinkedIn's "Contact info" panel, BenchPilot captures the contact details shown there. Those details are stored on your own computer (in your browser's local extension storage). They are never sent to BenchPilot servers or anyone else in readable form; with encrypted sync on (Solo) or an account backup, they are part of the locked copy described in section 2a.

1b. Resume files

Resumes (PDF or Word .docx) you attach to a consultant are stored on your own computer, in the extension's own browser database. BenchPilot reads their text on your computer, using a copy of the open-source pdf.js library bundled inside the extension for PDFs and its own built-in reader for Word files, only to suggest values for you to review. Removing a resume or deleting the consultant deletes the file.

  • Free, or Solo with sync off: resume files stay on your computer and are not uploaded.
  • Solo with sync on: your consultants' resume files are uploaded only encrypted, so we cannot read them (section 2a). Your own resume file, saved in Settings, is uploaded the same way, encrypted, so we cannot read it (section 2a).
  • Team workspace: a resume you add to a consultant in your team's workspace is stored with the team's book on our server, readable by the team's members (section 2b).

You can record a consultant's work-authorisation type and end date (for example H1B, until a given day), with a minimum rate and the date they are available. In the extension this stays on your computer (and in the encrypted copy only with encrypted sync on or an account backup); in a team workspace it is stored with the team's consultant record. BenchPilot never stores visa, passport, I-9 or other identity documents or their numbers: only the type and the end date, which it uses for reminders on the Today screen.

Your own profile details and the job openings you save in Jobs for you also stay on your computer; they are never sent to BenchPilot servers or anyone else in readable form, and are part of the locked copy only with encrypted sync on or an account backup (section 2a).

2. Where the data is stored

  • Everything BenchPilot finds and everything you save (leads, notes, statuses, follow-up dates, consultants, email templates and settings) is stored in your browser, using Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage). Unless encrypted sync or an account backup is on (section 2a), or you save to a team workspace (section 2b), it is stored only there.
  • Vendor book history. To keep your saved recruiters up to date, BenchPilot keeps, on your computer, the requirement details of the later posts it has read from each saved recruiter while you scroll (the same kind of details as the first saved post: title, skills, location, rate, date) and a history of the changes it made to a contact (for example a new title or a phone number that was missing), so you can see and undo each one. It never visits profiles, clicks or messages on LinkedIn to do this. Both are in your backups; the change history is part of each saved recruiter, so it is also in the encrypted sync copy if you turn sync on.
  • BenchPilot does not upload your personal leads, consultants, notes, templates or browsing data in readable form to the seller or to any third party. What reaches a server is: your account and payment information (section 5); with sync on (on by default for Solo) or an account backup you turn on, the encrypted sync copy or backup, and, if you create one, your personal inbox (sections 2a and 2d), which we cannot read; only if you belong to a team, what you save to the team's workspace and the team inbox (sections 2b and 2c); only when you press Send, a feedback report (section 5a); only if you turn it on, the anonymous error reports (section 5b); and only if you answer them, the two optional questions (section 5c).
  • The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only automatic reporting is the optional, off-by-default error reports in section 5b.

2a. Optional encrypted sync (Solo)

On the Solo plan (and the older Pro plan), Sync across computers is on by default: when you are signed in, BenchPilot sets it up and starts syncing, with nothing for you to do. Your BenchPilot data is then on every computer you sign in on, encrypted in your browser so only you can read it. You can turn it off at any time (see "Turning sync off" below). The Free plan has no sync; signed-in Free users can choose the optional account backup described below. If you do not sign in, nothing changes: there is no sync and no account backup, and your data stays only on your computer.

  • How it is locked: your computer makes a random data key, and your data is encrypted in your browser with it (AES-GCM, using Chrome's built-in WebCrypto) before anything leaves your computer. BenchPilot shows you a recovery code to save (Copy, Download or write it down); it always unlocks your data. A sync passphrase is optional: you can add one in Settings > Sync to unlock with something you remember. Your recovery code, your passphrase and the data key itself are never sent to us; we store the data key only in wrapped (locked) form.
  • What is stored on our server: encrypted copies of your data sets (leads, vendor companies, consultants, submissions, notes, saved jobs, templates, "not relevant" items and a short list of settings), and the technical values needed to unlock them on your computers: your data key in wrapped (encrypted) form, locked with your recovery code and, if you added a passphrase, with your passphrase; a key id; a key check value and a one-way hash of a key proof, so a computer can confirm it has the right key and only someone who holds the key can change it; and, only if you added a passphrase, a random salt and a passphrase check value (verifier). We also keep the size, the time and the computer id of each upload, so the copies stay in step.
  • Resume files: With sync on, your resume files are synced too, encrypted in your browser in the same way. Each file is encrypted in your browser with its own random key, and that key is locked with your data key, before the file leaves your computer. We store only the locked file and its size, never its name or contents, so we cannot read it.
  • Limits: your data sets can use up to 50 MB per account (5 MB per data set). Resume files are counted separately: up to 5 MB a file, 200 MB and 1,000 files per account. A file over the limit stays only on the computer where it is.
  • Approving another computer: to set up a new computer or the web app, you can approve it from a computer that already has sync. The new computer shows a short code; you type it on the computer that already has sync and press Approve, then confirm on the new computer. The new computer then receives your data key, locked so that only that new computer can open it. For this, our server briefly keeps a pairing request: the public keys and random numbers the two computers exchange, a short name for the new computer, and the one locked copy of the key, which we cannot open. A request expires after 10 minutes, the locked key is removed once the new computer collects it, and requests are deleted within a day.
  • Remember on this computer: so that you are not asked for your recovery code after every browser restart, a computer that set sync up, was approved, or was unlocked with "Remember on this computer" (ticked by default) keeps your data key on that computer, sealed with a browser key that web pages and other extensions cannot read. It is never in your backup files and never sent to us. It is not protected against someone who can read this computer's files (for example malware, or a stolen computer without disk encryption): with it and your sign-in they could also open your synced copy. Use disk encryption, and Forget the key on shared computers. It is used only for the same account. Forget the key on this computer in Settings > Sync removes it; turning sync off and deleting your account remove it too.
  • Who can read it: only you, on a computer that holds your key: one where you entered your recovery code or passphrase, one you approved from another of your computers, or one that remembers the key. We cannot read the synced data, and neither can Supabase. The admin console shows only whether sync is on, its size and the last sync time.
  • My book on the web: the BenchPilot web app can open your synced book in a browser tab. It downloads the locked copy and unlocks it in that tab with your recovery code, your passphrase if you added one, or by approving the tab from your computer; the key and the unlocked book are kept only in that tab's memory and are dropped when you sign out, close the tab or leave it idle for 30 minutes.
  • Where: our server on Supabase, project hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • What is not synced: your sign-in, licence and plan, usage counters, the error-report switch, display preferences (such as theme), undo history and sample data. These stay only on the computer where they are.
  • If you lose your recovery code and your passphrase (if you added one), and none of your computers still has the key: the synced copy cannot be recovered, by you or by us; we cannot reset it. The data already on each of your computers is not affected.
  • Turning sync off: in Settings > Sync, choose Turn off sync. This deletes the copy on our server, and BenchPilot remembers your choice for your account: sync does not turn itself on again until you press Turn on sync.
  • Retention: the synced copy is kept while sync is on. When you turn sync off, the copy on our server is deleted, resume files included. Your personal inbox (section 2d) is deleted too. If you delete your account, it is deleted too. If your Solo plan ends, sync pauses and the synced copy is deleted 60 days later (it stays if you renew within that time); an account backup, if you made one, is kept under its own rule below. The data on your computers is never touched.
  • Purpose: only to give you the same data on your computers. It is not used for anything else, never analysed and never sold.
  • Service provider: Supabase (database, file storage and server functions).

Account backup (Free, optional)

If you are signed in on the Free plan (or an older Basic plan), you can choose Back up to my account in Settings > Sync. It is off until you turn it on. It is not live sync: it is one saved copy (a snapshot) of your BenchPilot data, encrypted so only you can read it.

  • How it works: press Back up now, or turn on Back up automatically every week (off by default). The snapshot is encrypted in your browser with your own data key before it leaves your computer, the same way as sync (above): a recovery code that always works, an optional passphrase, and "Remember on this computer". We cannot read it.
  • What is in it: the same data as the backup file in Settings, without resume files (they are not uploaded). One snapshot per account, up to 5 MB encrypted; a new backup replaces the previous one.
  • What is stored on our server: the encrypted snapshot, its size and time, and the same key values as for sync (the wrapped data key, a key id, a key check value and a hash of the key proof; a salt and a verifier only if you add a passphrase). Where: Supabase, Mumbai, India.
  • Restoring: on a new computer, sign in, choose Restore from my account and unlock with your recovery code or approve it from your other computer. BenchPilot saves a safety backup of that computer first.
  • Retention: kept while your account exists, and deleted after 365 days without a new backup. Delete my account backup in Settings deletes it at once; deleting your account deletes it too. If you later buy Solo, sync starts with the same key.

2b. Team workspace

A company can use BenchPilot as a team: a shared workspace in the BenchPilot web app, which members can also save to from the extension. The team's data belongs to the company (the "customer"). For that data, the customer decides why and how it is used and we act on its instructions: the customer is the controller and BenchPilot is the processor.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the company is the data fiduciary for its consultants' and contacts' data and BenchPilot is its data processor (the data-processing terms are in section 7f of the Terms). The company answers its consultants' requests (a copy of their data, a correction, deletion, withdrawing consent); BenchPilot gives it the tools: "Export this consultant", and "Erase this consultant", which deletes their profile, resume files and notes and keeps the team's submission counts without their name.

  • What is stored for a team (entered, imported or saved by members): the team's consultants (name, title, skills, visa status (the work-authorisation type and end date, and a note; never a document), tax terms, rate and minimum rate, location, availability, status, notes and resume files, and consent records: the date, how it was given and a short note, and the location preference the consultant gave); contact notes your team types (calls, WhatsApp chats and meetings with consultants and vendor contacts; nothing is read from WhatsApp or a phone); consultant links (when an owner or manager made a link for one consultant, when it was opened and answered, and the answers the consultant gave: availability date, minimum rate, location preference, consent to be marketed and, for one submission, the right to represent); the vendor book (companies and recruiter contacts: name, email, phone, LinkedIn page address, title, tiers, notes, "do not contact" marks); saved requirements, including trimmed post text; submissions (consultant, vendor and the vendor chain to the end client, end client, rate, the right-to-represent (RTR) day, end date and agreed rate, bill rate, stage, dates); the pay rate of a submission, which only the owner and managers can see unless the owner lets recruiters see it; interviews (times and time zone, round, panel, notes, outcome) and placements (start and end date, bill rate, tax terms, PO number, status), with the placement pay rate under the same rule as the submission pay rate; timesheets (the week, regular and overtime hours, who approved them and the status); invoices made from them (number, period, hours, rates, amount, invoice, due and payment dates, status and a note) and the commission rules the owner or a manager sets (per member: a percentage of the margin or a fixed amount per placement, and a share for a lead), which only the owner and managers see; the team's invoice details (billing address, email, phone, tax ID, payment details and the invoice number prefix, printed on the team's invoices); the team inbox (section 2c); an activity log of who did what and when (by id, with no emails or phone numbers); targets the owner or a manager sets (submissions, interviews and placements per day, week or month, for the team, a desk or one member) and how often the duplicate check warned each member (a count per day), which owners and managers see and each member sees for themselves; and the members' names, emails, roles and invitations.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted. Team data is stored readable on our server (Supabase, Mumbai, India), encrypted at rest and in transit, because the service has to work on it (for example the team-wide "Already submitted?" check and the morning report). Row-level security keeps each team's data visible only to that team's members. Resume files sit in private storage with short-lived links. Only the founder has admin access, and looks at team data only to give support the team asked for or where the law requires it.
  • Roles: the owner (one at a time; ownership can be transferred), managers and recruiters. Every member sees the team's book. The owner decides whether managers and recruiters may export it. Removing a member ends their access at once; what they added stays with the team.
  • Consultant links. An owner or manager can send one consultant a private link (it ends after 14 days and can be cancelled) to confirm their availability, minimum rate, location preference and consent, and to give or decline the right to represent for one submission. The consultant needs no account. The page shows only the firm's name, the consultant's first name and, for the right to represent, the role and end client; nothing else from the team's data. We store only a fingerprint of the link, never the link itself. To limit abuse we keep a keyed fingerprint of the visitor's IP address for at most one day, never the address. The answers are saved on the consultant's record in the team's data; erasing the consultant deletes their links and answers.
  • Company sign-in rule. The owner can restrict who joins the workspace to one email domain (for example the company's own). We store the domain in the workspace settings.
  • Two books. Your personal book (Free or Solo) stays yours. Joining a team does not copy it; what you choose to save to the team then belongs to the company. Please tell your members that the company can see what they save to the team.
  • Pilot and closing: a new workspace starts with a 14-day pilot (no card; one 14-day extension on request). If the pilot or a paid plan ends without payment, the workspace becomes read-only for 30 days (export still works) and is then closed. The owner can also delete the workspace at any time.
  • Deletion for good: a closed workspace is deleted for good 30 days after it closes. We email the owner a warning at least 7 days before. Everything in it is deleted: the book, resume files, inbox, activity log, members and invitations. We keep only a record that a team with that id was deleted, when and why, with row counts (no names, emails or content), payment records (section 5) and the members' own accounts and Solo data, which are not the team's.
  • Team emails (Brevo): invitations, the daily and weekly morning report (counts and the names of the company's own consultants and recruiters, never vendor emails or phone numbers), and plan and closing notices to the owner.

2c. Team inbox

A team on a plan that includes it can create a team inbox address and forward requirement emails to it.

  • How mail arrives: email sent to the address is received by Cloudflare Email Routing and passed to our server. We store the sender, subject, date and text (cut to 20,000 characters). Attachments are not stored.
  • Who can read it: the members of that team. It is stored readable, like the rest of the team's data (section 2b).
  • Retention: each message is deleted 90 days after it arrives, and a message marked ignored is deleted 30 days after it was ignored. A requirement a member made from a message stays in the team's book. Turning the address off stops new mail.
  • Only forward mail you have the right to share with your team (see the Terms).

2d. Personal inbox (Solo)

With Solo sync on, you can create a personal inbox address and forward requirement emails to it.

  • Encrypted on arrival: email sent to the address is received by Cloudflare Email Routing and passed to our server, which encrypts it at once to your personal inbox key and stores only the encrypted message. The key that opens it is locked with your sync data key, so only you can read the stored messages. Like any email, a message is readable while it travels to us and while our server encrypts it; it is never stored readable.
  • What we can see: the arrival time, size and status of each message, never its sender, subject or text.
  • Retention: each message is deleted 90 days after it arrives, and a message marked ignored 30 days after it was ignored. The whole inbox is deleted when you turn sync off or delete your account.

2e. The web app on your computer

The BenchPilot web app (the team workspace and My book) keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: your sign-in session, your theme choice and, if you opened a "Bring BenchPilot to your team" link, its code (for up to 30 days, until a workspace is created). A team invitation link is kept only for the browser session. It uses no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising code. Your unlocked Solo book is never written to the browser's storage (section 2a).

3. We do not sell or transfer your data

We do not sell, rent, trade or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to BenchPilot's single purpose (helping you find and manage recruiter leads and pitch consultants), and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. BenchPilot uses no AI, and your data is never used to train any model.

4. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

BenchPilot's use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Accounts, license keys and payments

From version 1.14 (November 2026), setting up BenchPilot includes creating a free account (email and password with a 6-digit email code, or "Continue with Google"). The account holds your plan; your recruiters, consultants and submissions still stay in your browser (and, with Solo sync on or an account backup, in the encrypted copy described in section 2a). For the account, the following is handled:

  • Sign-in and account database (Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India): your email address and either a securely hashed password (we never see or store your password in readable form) or your Google account identity (email, name and Google user id; we never receive your Google password). Supabase also keeps sign-in technical data such as timestamps.
  • Our server functions and database (Supabase): your account email, your account user id, your plan dates, your team membership and role, and payment records: Razorpay payment and payment-link ids, plan, amount and currency. They also receive the sign-in token your extension sends when it checks your plan.
  • Emails (Brevo): account emails (confirmation and password reset), team invitations, team morning reports and plan or closing notices are sent through Brevo, a transactional email provider, which processes the email address and the message only to deliver it.
  • Payments (Razorpay): you pay on a Razorpay page (UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets). Razorpay processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Purpose: only to sign you in, activate and verify your plan on your account across your computers, run your team's workspace, handle duplicate or failed payment reversals, send account and team emails, provide support, and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • Retention: account data is kept while your account exists. Payment records (payment ids, plan, amount, currency and dates) are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, currently up to 8 years, even if the account is deleted; when you delete your account, they are kept without your user id, so they no longer point to your name or email. Razorpay keeps its own record of the payment under its own policy.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account yourself (section 7).
  • Your plan is delivered to the extension as a digitally signed license, which the extension verifies offline. Older license keys issued by email continue to work the same way.
  • Messages you send us by email are handled by your email provider under its own privacy policy.

Service providers (sub-processors)

  • Supabase: sign-in, database, file storage and server functions; project hosted in Mumbai, India. Holds account data, team data, the encrypted Solo copy and inbox, and feedback.

Your personal leads, consultants, notes, templates and settings are never sent to our servers or to these providers in readable form; with Solo sync on or an account backup, only the encrypted copy in section 2a is stored, and we cannot read it.

5a. Feedback and bug reports

BenchPilot has a Report a bug / Send feedback button (in the LinkedIn drawer, the popup, the dashboard and Settings, and the Alt+Shift+F shortcut). Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

  • What is sent: the message you type, whether it is a Bug or an Idea, and a small fixed set of details: the page address (without any query string or fragment; on LinkedIn only the path, for example "/feed/"), which BenchPilot screen you were on (drawer, popup, dashboard view or settings), the extension version, your plan (Free, Solo, or an older Basic or Pro plan), your browser and operating system in short form (for example "Chrome 130 / Windows"), your language and the time. If you are signed in, your account email and user id are attached so we can reply to you.
  • Never sent: your leads, contacts, consultants, notes, templates, resumes or any other data from your browser storage.
  • Optional screenshot: only if you tick "Include a screenshot of this screen". The picture shows what is on your screen, which may include other people's names and posts on LinkedIn. It is off by default. If you do not tick it, no picture is taken. You can untick it at any time before sending.
  • Where it goes: to our server on Supabase (hosted in Mumbai, India). Reports are stored in a private database table and screenshots in a private storage area that is not publicly accessible. Only the founder can read them, through a protected admin console.
  • If you are offline: the report is kept on your computer (up to 20) and sent automatically when you are back online. Unsent reports stay only in your browser.
  • Spam protection: to limit abuse we keep a salted one-way hash of your account id (or, if you are not signed in, of your IP address and browser). We do not store your raw IP address.
  • Purpose: to fix bugs, answer you and improve BenchPilot. Reports are not used for advertising and are never sold.
  • Retention: reports and their screenshots are deleted after 12 months. If you delete your account sooner, your reports are kept as anonymous reports, with your email and user id removed.
  • Deletion: to have a report or screenshot deleted sooner, email nicklennox30@gmail.com from your account email (or quote the time you sent it).
  • Service provider: Supabase (database and storage).

5b. Anonymous error reports (optional, off by default)

In Settings > Error reports (also in the gear menu of the LinkedIn drawer) there is a switch, Send anonymous error reports. It is off by default. While it is off, BenchPilot collects nothing and sends nothing about errors.

  • What is sent, if you turn it on: when part of BenchPilot breaks, the name of the error, a short error message (up to 300 characters), where in BenchPilot's own code it happened (file names and line numbers only), the BenchPilot version, which screen it happened on (background worker, dashboard, popup, LinkedIn drawer or settings), your Chrome version number (for example "Chrome 130") and the time. Email-like text, phone-like numbers and anything after a "?" in a web address are removed before sending.
  • Never sent: your leads, contacts, consultants, notes, templates, resumes, LinkedIn pages or addresses, page text, your email address or any account or user id. Error reports are not linked to your account or to you, and no screenshot is ever included.
  • Limits: at most 5 error reports an hour from your computer, and the same error is sent only once in 24 hours. If you are offline, reports wait on your computer and are sent later; turning the switch off discards any that are waiting.
  • Where it goes and how long we keep it: to the same private database table as feedback reports on Supabase (Mumbai, India), readable only by the founder through the protected admin console. They follow the same retention as feedback reports: deleted after 12 months. To limit abuse we keep a salted one-way hash of your IP address and browser for the hourly limit, never the raw address.
  • Capture warning: if BenchPilot can see requirement posts but cannot read any recruiter emails, the drawer shows a note with a Report it button. When the switch is on, that report holds counts only (how many requirement posts had emails and how many were read), plus the usual error details above, and never the posts or emails themselves.
  • Purpose: to find and fix bugs. Not used for advertising, never sold.
  • How to turn it off: switch it off in Settings > Error reports (or in the drawer's gear menu) at any time. It takes effect immediately.
  • Service provider: Supabase (database).

5c. Two optional questions

BenchPilot asks two short questions. Both are optional, and nothing is sent unless you answer.

  • "How did you hear about BenchPilot?" on the welcome page after you install. If you pick an answer, BenchPilot sends that answer and its version number, once. It is not linked to your account, even if you are signed in.

If that first payment is fully refunded, the reward is taken back. If you delete your account, a reward already given is kept as an anonymous record (without your user id); open links are deleted.

  • Delete your account yourself: open Account in the extension (or in the web app), choose Delete my account and type your account email to confirm. This deletes your sign-in, your plan, your encrypted synced copy and resume files, your account backup, and your personal inbox, straight away.

8. Retention at a glance

  • Data on your computer: until you delete it or uninstall the extension.
  • Account: while your account exists.
  • Payment records: up to 8 years (tax and accounting law), without your user id once you delete your account.
  • Solo synced copy and resume files: until you turn sync off or delete your account; 60 days after your Solo plan ends.
  • Account backup (Free, optional): while your account exists; deleted after 365 days without a new backup, or when you delete it.
  • Device pairing requests: expire after 10 minutes; deleted within a day.
  • Personal inbox: 90 days per message (30 days after you ignore one); all of it when sync is turned off.
  • Team workspace: while the team has a pilot or paid plan; then 30 days read-only, then closed; deleted for good 30 days after closing, with a warning to the owner at least 7 days before.
  • Team inbox: 90 days per message (30 days after it is ignored).
  • Free team pilot record: a one-way hash of your email is kept to stop repeat free pilots, also after you delete your account. It is used only to check whether an email has already had a free pilot.
  • Feedback, optional answers and error reports: 12 months.
