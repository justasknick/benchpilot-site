FAQ

Pricing questions

Something else? Write to us.

What counts as a consultant?

A person on your bench: a profile you add so BenchPilot can match requirements to it. Free holds 3 and Solo up to 200. Recruiter contacts are not consultants: they are unlimited on every plan.

What happens when my Solo plan ends?

You go back to Free. Your data stays in your browser and Solo features pause until you pay again. If sync was on, the paused synced copy is deleted 60 days after the plan ends, and we email you before that.

How does the 14-day team pilot work?

Start it in the BenchPilot web app with no card. Every feature is on for 14 days, and one more fortnight is available on request. If the team does not pay when the pilot ends, the workspace turns read-only for 30 days, so you can export everything, and is then closed. Nothing is ever charged on its own.

Can I move from Solo to Team?

Yes. It is the same BenchPilot and the same account: start a team pilot in the web app and invite your recruiters. They keep using the Chrome extension, and your Solo plan stays yours until it ends.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets on the Razorpay page. Checkout is in rupees for now; cards issued abroad pay the rupee price. Teams that pay in US dollars are invoiced until dollar payment is available in the web app.

Do I get a receipt?

Razorpay confirms each payment on its page and by email or SMS. If you need a receipt for your accounts, email us with your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_).

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are final, so please try Free or a team pilot first. If you were charged twice, or paid and your plan did not switch on, contact us within 7 days and we will fix it. See the refund policy.
