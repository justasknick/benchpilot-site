Team workspace and inbox

How a BenchPilot team workspace starts, who can do what, how the team inbox works and how to take your data with you.

The 14-day pilot

  • Create a workspace in the BenchPilot web app. It starts with a 14-day pilot with every feature on and no card.
  • Need more time? The owner can ask us for one 14-day extension.
  • To keep going, the owner picks a plan (Team · 5 up to 5 people, Team · 15 up to 15, Team · 40 up to 40; members plus pending invitations, the owner included) and pays with Razorpay. Each payment adds its months to the team's paid-until date. There is no auto-renewal.
  • Changing plan (for example Team · 5 to Team · 15): the paid days left convert by price to the new plan, then the new months are added. If the plan has already ended, the new plan starts from the day you pay. Days left in a pilot carry over in full.
  • If nobody pays, the workspace turns read-only for 30 days (everyone can still see and export the book), then it is closed. A closed workspace is deleted for good 30 days later; the owner gets a warning email at least 7 days before.

Roles

  • Owner (one at a time): everything, plus billing, transferring ownership and deleting the workspace.
  • Manager: invites and removes members, changes roles (not the owner's), sets vendor tiers.
  • Recruiter: works in the bench, vendor book, requirements and submissions.
  • The owner decides whether managers and recruiters may export the book.
  • Paid plans have a people limit: Team · 5, Team · 15 and Team · 40 hold 5, 15 and 40 people (members plus pending invitations). The Team · 5 bench holds up to 25 consultants.

The team inbox (forwarding address)

  1. In the web app, open Inbox and create an address. It looks like r-xxxxxxxxxxxx@ followed by BenchPilot's inbox domain. Team · 15 has 1 address, Team · 40 has 3, the pilot 1; Team · 5 has none.
  2. In Gmail or Outlook, set up forwarding (or a filter that forwards requirement emails) to that address.
  3. Gmail sends a confirmation email first. It appears pinned at the top of the BenchPilot Inbox with its code or link, so you can confirm forwarding.
  4. Forwarded emails appear in the Inbox. Press Add to requirements to turn one into a requirement, or ignore it.
  • Messages are deleted 90 days after they arrive, and ignored ones 30 days after they were ignored. Requirements made from them stay.
  • Only forward mail you have the right to share with your team. Attachments are not stored.
  • Solo users have a separate personal inbox that is encrypted on arrival (see Encrypted sync).

Export

The owner can export the whole team book (consultants, vendor companies, vendor contacts, requirements and submissions) at any time, including during the read-only period. Managers and recruiters can export if the owner allows it.

Leaving a team

  • A member leaves when the owner or a manager removes them. Access ends at once; what they added stays with the company.
  • Your personal book (Free or Solo) is yours and is not affected.
  • An owner who wants to leave transfers ownership to another member first (the old owner becomes a manager), or deletes the workspace.
  • Deleting your own account also removes you from the team (see Deleting your account).
