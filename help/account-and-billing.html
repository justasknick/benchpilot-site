Account and billing

How paying, renewing, changing plan and deleting your BenchPilot account work.

Buying Solo

Open Plans in the extension, sign in, pick Solo monthly or yearly and pay on the Razorpay page (UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets). Your plan switches on by itself on every computer where you sign in. Solo is ₹499 a month or ₹3,999 a year; the founding offer is ₹399 a month for the first 100 buyers, kept for 12 months from the first founding payment.

Renewing and extending

  • There is no auto-renewal. Every payment is a one-time payment; we never charge you again on our own.
  • To renew or extend, pay again from Plans. Paying before your plan ends adds the new period to the time you have left, so you never lose days.
  • We email you about 7 days and again 1 day before a paid plan ends. If sync was on, we email again about 7 days before the paused synced copy is deleted (60 days after the plan ends).
  • When your plan ends, you go back to Free. Your data stays in your browser; Solo features pause until you renew.

Basic and Pro

Basic and Pro are no longer sold. If you have one, you keep it, with the limits it had, until the period you paid for ends. You can then choose Solo or return to Free. If you buy Solo while an older plan still has days left, those days convert to Solo days by price, rounded down (for example, 30 days of Pro become 36 days of Solo; 30 days of Basic become 17 days of Solo).

Receipts

Razorpay confirms each payment on its page and by email or SMS to the contact you give it. Keep your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_). If you need a receipt for your accounts, email us with that ID.

A payment went wrong

Charged twice, or paid and the plan did not switch on? Make sure you are signed in to the account you paid from, reopen the extension and wait a few minutes. If the plan has not appeared within 15 minutes, email us within 7 days with your account email and Razorpay payment ID. See the Refund policy and Shipping and delivery.

Deleting your account

  1. Open Account in the extension (or in the web app) and choose Delete my account.
  2. Type your account email to confirm.
  • Deleted at once: your sign-in, your plan, your encrypted synced copy and resume files, and your personal inbox.
  • If you own a team, transfer ownership or delete the workspace first. If you are a member, you leave the team; what you added stays with the team.
  • Kept: payment records for tax and accounting, without your user id; feedback you sent, as anonymous reports; and, if you ever started a team pilot, a one-way hash of your email (never the email itself), kept to stop repeat free pilots.
  • The data on your computer is not touched. Uninstall the extension, or clear it in the dashboard, to remove it.
  • Cannot sign in? Email us from your account email and we will delete it for you.
