7a. Optional encrypted sync (Solo)

  • Sync is included in Solo (and the older Pro plan) and turns on automatically when you are signed in; you can turn it off in Settings. Your data is then on every computer you sign in on, encrypted in your browser with your own key, so only you can read it. A sync passphrase is optional; your recovery code always unlocks your data. We store a locked copy (Supabase, Mumbai, India) that we cannot read. Your resume files are synced too, encrypted in your browser in the same way; turning sync off deletes them from our server.
  • You are responsible for your recovery code and, if you add one, your sync passphrase. We never receive them and cannot reset them. If you lose them and none of your computers still has the key, the synced copy cannot be recovered by you or by us. Approve a new computer only when you are setting it up yourself. The data already on each of your computers is not affected.
  • Turning sync off deletes the copy on our server, and your personal inbox with it; sync then stays off for your account until you turn it on again. If your Solo plan ends, sync pauses and the copy on our server is deleted 60 days later, unless you renew before then. We email you about 7 days and again 1 day before a paid plan ends, and about 7 days before a paused copy is deleted. Your local data is never touched.
  • Sync is not a backup service: a change or deletion on one computer reaches your other computers. Keep the free backup file for anything important.
  • Sync limits: your data can use up to 50 MB per account (5 MB per data set). Resume files are counted separately: up to 5 MB a file, 200 MB and 1,000 files per account. A file over the limit stays only on the computer where it is.
  • Account backup (Free, optional): signed-in users without Solo can turn on Back up to my account. It keeps one encrypted snapshot of your data, up to 5 MB encrypted, without resume files. It is not sync. It is kept while your account exists and deleted after 365 days without a new backup, or when you delete it. If a Solo plan ends, the synced copy is still deleted 60 days later, but an account backup is kept under this rule.
