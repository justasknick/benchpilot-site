How it works

From LinkedIn post to pitch in three steps

  1. Browse LinkedIn as usual

    Open a LinkedIn search or feed. BenchPilot reads the visible recruiter posts and pulls out the email, role, location, skills and phone.

  2. Review in the side drawer

    Leads appear in a drawer next to the page. Your bench consultants are matched to each requirement by skills and title.

  3. Pitch in one click

    Hit Pitch or Submit. Your email opens in Gmail or Outlook, pre-filled from your template. The lead is saved to your private dashboard.

FAQ

Questions

How do I get Pro?

Open the Upgrade page in the extension, sign in with your email or Google, pick a plan and pay on Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets). Pro activates instantly on your account. There is no key to paste. If Pro does not appear within 15 minutes, contact support with your Razorpay payment id. If you already have a license key from us, it still works: paste it on the Upgrade page.

What happens when my plan ends?

You go back to the Free plan. Your saved leads and consultants stay in your browser and nothing is deleted. Pro features (unlimited saves, Auto-Scroller, export, follow-ups) pause until you renew.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your leads, consultants and settings are stored only in your browser and never uploaded. Only your account email, plan dates and payment records are kept on our account server (Supabase, Mumbai, India). Read the privacy policy.

Can I use it on 2 computers?

Yes. Sign in to your BenchPilot account on any computer and Pro is there too. Your leads stay local to each browser.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. BenchPilot never sees your card or UPI details.

Refunds?

No. All Pro purchases are final. Please use the Free plan to try BenchPilot first. If you were charged twice or Pro didn't activate, contact us and we'll fix it. Details are in the refund policy.

Is BenchPilot affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. BenchPilot is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You are responsible for following LinkedIn's terms and the email laws that apply to you.
