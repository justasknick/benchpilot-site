Questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your vendor book, consultants and settings stay in your browser. If you create an account, we keep only your sign-in, plan and payment details. Read the privacy policy.

Does BenchPilot send emails for me?

No. It opens a draft in Gmail or Outlook. You check the recipient, resume and message, then press Send yourself.

What does “Already submitted?” check?

Your saved submission history in this browser. If you sent the same consultant to the same client in the last 90 days, you see a warning before you draft.

Is it affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. BenchPilot is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You remain responsible for following LinkedIn’s terms.
