What the drawer does

The drawer docks beside LinkedIn and fills as you scroll. Everything below is a real screen, with sample data.

Why this consultant fits

Each match is scored from 0 to 100. Open the card to see the skills that matched, the ones missing, and blockers such as a visa the post rules out.

Only matches of 60% or more are shown. The score orders the list; you decide.

Already submitted? Checked before you send

If you sent the same consultant to the same end client in the last 90 days, the card says so in red: where, through which vendor, and when.

It checks the submissions recorded in BenchPilot, so record each one and the check stays sharp.

Every recruiter, one tap away

The recruiter from each post sits on its card with email, phone, WhatsApp and LinkedIn profile. Copy, call or open a chat without leaving the feed.

Filter the day’s requirements by C2C, remote, matches or saved.

Vendors you can trust, and the ones you avoid

Mark each vendor Trusted, Neutral or Avoid, and set its tier: prime vendor, implementation partner or direct client.

A vendor you marked Avoid never gets the loud purple button.

Your bench at a glance

Days on the bench, this week’s submissions and interviews, and the best match today for each consultant.

Add a consultant by dropping a PDF or Word résumé. It is read on your computer.

The draft, never the send

Draft email opens Gmail or Outlook with the consultant, the rate and your right-to-represent note ready. You read it and press Send.

BenchPilot logs it as submitted, with a follow-up date if you set one.

What BenchPilot never does

BenchPilot reads only what your browser already shows you. It never clicks, scrolls or sends a message on LinkedIn, so your account behaves exactly as it does without it.

Your recruiters, consultants, résumés and submissions stay in your browser. If you create an account, we keep only your sign-in, plan and payment details.

There is no AI inside. Matches come from rules you can read: skills, title, location, visa and rate.

It never sends an email. It opens a draft, and you press Send.

Back up everything to one file whenever you like. The backup is free on every plan.

Questions

Does BenchPilot send emails for me? No. It opens a draft in Gmail or Outlook. You review the recipient, résumé and message, then press Send yourself.

What does the “Already submitted?” check look at? Your saved BenchPilot submission history in this browser. The same consultant and a known end client within 90 days triggers a warning. Missing client names, unrecorded submissions and teammates’ activity are outside it. Vendors you marked Avoid get a “Draft email anyway” button.

What counts as a contact saved this month? Saving, copying or pitching a new recruiter. The same recruiter again is free. Free has 25 a month, Basic 300, Pro is unlimited under fair use. After that, new contacts show masked, such as r***@company.com, until next month or an upgrade.

Is my data uploaded anywhere? No. Leads, consultants, captured contact details and settings stay in your browser. With an account, your sign-in, plan and payment details are kept on our account server (Supabase, Mumbai). Feedback is sent only when you send it. Read the privacy policy.

What can I do from the Recruiters tab? Every saved recruiter with LinkedIn, email, phone and WhatsApp one click away, plus a plain email draft. Filter by Trust, Vendor tier, status, or a missing phone, profile or vendor.

What are Trust and Vendor tier? Two separate labels on each vendor. Trust is Trusted, Neutral or Avoid. Vendor tier is where the vendor sits, such as prime vendor or implementation partner.

What is the autosave guard? Autosave uses one monthly contact for each new recruiter it saves. With 3 left it turns itself off, and you can turn it on anyway. Pro is never paused, and saving by hand is not affected.

How do I buy Basic or Pro? Open the Upgrade page in the extension, sign in with email or Google, pick a plan and pay on Razorpay with UPI or a card. If the plan does not appear within 15 minutes, write to support with your Razorpay payment ID. An older licence key still works on the Upgrade page.

Can I extend my plan early? Yes. In the last 14 days of Basic or Pro, the Upgrade page offers Extend, and the days you have left are kept.

What happens when my plan ends? You go back to Free. Nothing is deleted. Higher contact limits, extra consultants and templates, follow-up dates, exports and, for Pro, the tech matcher pause until you renew.

What is the founding offer? The first 50 buyers can get Pro at ₹399 a month instead of ₹599, while founding places last.

Can I use it on two computers? Yes. Sign in on each and your plan is there. Your leads stay in each browser.

What does the profile card read? Only the profile you are viewing. For 1st-degree connections, it keeps the details LinkedIn shows when you open Contact info. Nothing is read in the background.

Is payment secure? Payments go through Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. BenchPilot never sees your card or UPI details.

Refunds? All purchases are final, so please use Free first. If you were charged twice or your plan did not switch on, contact us and we will fix it. See the refund policy.

Is BenchPilot affiliated with LinkedIn? No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You remain responsible for following LinkedIn’s terms and the email laws that apply to you.
