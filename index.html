Questions

Does BenchPilot send emails for me?

No. It opens a draft in Gmail or Outlook. You review the recipient, résumé and message, then press Send yourself.

What does the “Already submitted?” check look at?

Your saved BenchPilot submission history in this browser. The same consultant and a known end client within 90 days triggers a warning. Missing client names, unrecorded submissions and teammates’ activity are outside it. Vendors you marked Avoid get a “Draft email anyway” button.

What counts as a contact saved this month?

Saving, copying or pitching a new recruiter. The same recruiter again is free. Free has 25 a month, Basic 300, and Pro is unlimited under fair use. After that, new contacts show masked, such as r***@company.com, until next month or an upgrade.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Leads, consultants, captured contact details and settings stay in your browser. With an account, your sign-in, plan and payment details are kept on our account server (Supabase, Mumbai). Feedback is sent only when you send it. Read the privacy policy.

How do I buy Basic or Pro?

Open the Upgrade page in the extension, sign in with email or Google, pick a plan and pay on Razorpay with UPI or a card. If the plan does not appear within 15 minutes, write to support with your Razorpay payment ID. An older licence key still works on the Upgrade page.

What are Trust and Vendor tier?

Two separate labels on each vendor. Trust is Trusted, Neutral or Avoid. Vendor tier is where the vendor sits, such as prime vendor or implementation partner.

What is the autosave guard?

Autosave uses one monthly contact for each new recruiter it saves. With 3 left it turns itself off, and you can turn it on anyway. Pro is never paused, and saving by hand is not affected.

Can I extend my plan early?

Yes. In the last 14 days of Basic or Pro, the Upgrade page offers Extend, and the days you have left are kept.

What happens when my plan ends?

You go back to Free. Nothing is deleted. Higher contact limits, extra consultants and templates, follow-up dates, exports and, for Pro, the tech matcher pause until you renew.

What is the founding offer?

The first 50 buyers can get Pro at ₹399 a month instead of ₹599, while founding places last.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes. Sign in on each and your plan is there. Your leads stay in each browser.

What does the profile card read?

Only the profile you are viewing. For 1st-degree connections, it keeps the details LinkedIn shows when you open Contact info. Nothing is read in the background.

Is payment secure?

Payments go through Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. BenchPilot never sees your card or UPI details.

Refunds?

All purchases are final, so please use Free first. If you were charged twice or your plan did not switch on, contact us and we will fix it. See the refund policy.

Is BenchPilot affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You remain responsible for following LinkedIn’s terms and the email laws that apply to you.
