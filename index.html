FAQ

Questions

More in the help centre, or write to us.

Is it safe for my LinkedIn account?

BenchPilot reads only what the browser already shows on the pages you open. It never clicks, scrolls or messages on LinkedIn, and it never sends an email for you: it opens a draft and you press Send. You remain responsible for following LinkedIn’s terms.

Where is my data stored?

On Free, your vendor book, consultants and settings stay in your browser and are not uploaded, unless you turn on the optional encrypted account backup. On Solo, encrypted sync is on by default (you can turn it off): your book is then on every computer you sign in on, locked in your browser with your own key, so only you can read it. We keep a locked copy we cannot open. On Free your resume files stay on your computer; on Solo with sync on they are encrypted in your browser and synced too, we cannot read them, and turning sync off deletes them from our server. If you create an account, we keep your sign-in, plan and payment details. Read the privacy policy.

What does Free include?

Unlimited recruiter contacts from requirement posts, your vendor book, 3 consultants, 1 email template, matching with the reasons, the “Already submitted?” check and a backup file. Free has no time limit and needs no card.

What does Solo add?

Up to 200 consultants and 10 email templates, follow-up dates, the hotlist builder, the tech matcher, saved smart lists, Today’s homework extras, weekly targets, placement margins, invoices from your timesheets with a commission estimate, export to CSV and Excel, and encrypted sync with a personal inbox address and your book in the web app. Buy it inside BenchPilot: open Plans, sign in and pay with Razorpay. See pricing.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

There is no auto-renewal: every payment is one-time, so there is nothing to cancel. When a plan ends you go back to Free and keep your data. All purchases are final, so please try Free first; if you were charged twice or your plan did not switch on, we fix it. See the refund policy.

Is BenchPilot linked to LinkedIn?

No. BenchPilot is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation.
