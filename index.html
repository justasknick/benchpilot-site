FAQ

Questions

What can I do from the Recruiters tab?

Each saved recruiter has their LinkedIn profile, email, phone and WhatsApp one click away, plus a plain Email draft. Filter by Trust, Vendor type, status, or missing phone, LinkedIn or vendor. In the drawer, copying a new contact still uses a reveal.

What are Trust and Vendor type?

Two separate labels on each vendor. Trust is Trusted, Neutral or Avoid. Vendor type is where the vendor sits, for example Prime or Implementation partner. Setting one does not change the other.

What is the autosave guard?

Autosave uses 1 reveal for each new recruiter it saves. When you have 3 reveals left, it turns itself off, and you can choose "Turn on anyway". Pro is never paused, and saving by hand is not affected.

Can I extend my plan early?

Yes. In the last 14 days of a Basic or Pro plan, the Upgrade page offers Extend. The days you have left are kept and the new end date is shown first.

Does BenchPilot send emails for me?

No. It opens a draft in Gmail or Outlook. You review the recipient, résumé and message, then press Send yourself.

What can the duplicate guard check?

It checks saved BenchPilot submission history in this browser. A matching consultant and known end client within 90 days can trigger a warning. Missing client details, unrecorded submissions and teammates' activity are outside that check. Vendors you marked Avoid also trigger a warning, with a "Submit anyway" option.

How do I buy Basic or Pro?

Open the Upgrade page in the extension, sign in with your email or Google, pick Basic or Pro (monthly or yearly) and pay on Razorpay with UPI or a card. Your plan is linked to your account and works on every computer where you sign in. If it does not appear within 15 minutes, contact support with your Razorpay payment id. If you already have a license key from us, it still works: paste it on the Upgrade page.

What is a reveal?

A reveal is saving, copying or pitching a new recruiter contact. Free gives you 25 a month, Basic 300 and Pro is unlimited under fair use. After your monthly reveals are used, new contacts show masked, such as r***@company.com, until next month or until you upgrade. Contacts you already revealed stay visible.

What happens when my plan ends?

You go back to the Free plan. Your saved leads and consultants stay in your browser and nothing is deleted. Paid features pause until you renew: higher reveal limits, extra consultants and templates, follow-up dates on any recruiter, CSV export, and for Pro JSON export and tech matcher.

Which features are Pro only?

Pro adds the following on top of Basic:

  • Unlimited reveals (fair use)
  • Up to 200 consultants
  • 10 templates
  • JSON export of leads
  • Tech matcher
What is the founding offer?

The first 50 buyers can get Pro at ₹399 a month instead of ₹599. It is offered while founding spots last.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your leads, consultants, captured LinkedIn contact details and settings are stored only in your browser and never uploaded. If you create an account, your sign-in, plan and payment information is kept on our account server (Supabase, Mumbai, India). Feedback reports are sent only when you choose to submit them. Read the privacy policy.

What does the LinkedIn profile card read?

Only the profile page you are viewing. For your 1st-degree connections, it captures the contact details LinkedIn shows when you open Contact info. Nothing is read in the background, and the details stay on your computer.

Can I use it on 2 computers?

Yes. Sign in to your BenchPilot account on any computer and your plan is there too. Your leads stay local to each browser.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. BenchPilot never sees your card or UPI details.

Refunds?

No. All Basic and Pro purchases are final, so please use the Free plan first. If you were charged twice or your plan did not activate, contact us and we will fix it. Details are in the refund policy.

No. BenchPilot is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You are responsible for following LinkedIn's terms and the email laws that apply to you.
