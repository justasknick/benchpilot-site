How it works

From LinkedIn post to pitch in three steps

  1. Browse LinkedIn as usual

    Open a LinkedIn search or feed. BenchPilot reads the visible recruiter posts and pulls out the role, location, skills, email and phone.

  2. Review in the side drawer

    Leads appear in a drawer next to the page, with US times converted to IST. Your bench consultants are matched to each requirement by skills and title.

  3. Pitch in one click

    Hit Pitch or Submit. Your email opens in Gmail or Outlook, pre-filled from your template. The lead is saved to your private dashboard.

FAQ

Questions

How do I buy Basic or Pro?

Open the Upgrade page in the extension, sign in with your email or Google, pick Basic or Pro (monthly or yearly) and pay on Razorpay with UPI or a card. Your plan activates on your account within seconds and works on every computer where you sign in. If it does not appear within 15 minutes, contact support with your Razorpay payment id. If you already have a license key from us, it still works: paste it on the Upgrade page.

What is a reveal?

A reveal is saving, copying or pitching a new recruiter contact. Free gives you 25 a month, Basic 300 and Pro is unlimited under fair use. After your monthly reveals are used, new contacts show masked, such as r***@company.com, until next month or until you upgrade. Contacts you already revealed stay visible.

What happens when my plan ends?

You go back to the Free plan. Your saved leads and consultants stay in your browser and nothing is deleted. Paid features pause until you renew: higher reveal limits, extra consultants and templates, follow-up reminders, CSV export, and for Pro the Auto-Scroller, JSON backup export and tech matcher.

Which features are Pro only?

Pro adds the following on top of Basic:

  • Unlimited reveals (fair use)
  • Up to 200 consultants
  • 10 templates
  • Auto-Scroller
  • JSON backup export
  • Tech matcher
What is the founding offer?

The first 50 buyers can get Pro at ₹399 a month instead of ₹599. It is offered while founding spots last.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your leads, consultants, captured LinkedIn contact details and settings are stored only in your browser and never uploaded. If you create an account, only your account email, plan dates and payment records are kept on our account server (Supabase, Mumbai, India). Read the privacy policy.

What does the LinkedIn profile card read?

Only the profile page you are viewing. For your 1st-degree connections, it captures the contact details LinkedIn shows when you open Contact info. Nothing is read in the background, and the details stay on your computer.

Can I use it on 2 computers?

Yes. Sign in to your BenchPilot account on any computer and your plan is there too. Your leads stay local to each browser.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. BenchPilot never sees your card or UPI details.

Refunds?

No. All Basic and Pro purchases are final, so please use the Free plan first. If you were charged twice or your plan did not activate, contact us and we will fix it. Details are in the refund policy.

Is BenchPilot affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. BenchPilot is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. You are responsible for following LinkedIn's terms and the email laws that apply to you.
