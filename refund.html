Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14 · Operated by Rakessh.D

All purchases of BenchPilot Solo, of team plans (Team · 5, Team · 15 and Team · 40) and of the older Basic and Pro plans are final and non-refundable. Please try the Free plan, or a 14-day team pilot, before you buy.

No refunds

All purchases of BenchPilot Solo, including the founding offer, of team plans and of the older Basic and Pro plans are final and non-refundable, including partially used periods. Every Free feature is available at no cost, so we encourage you to use the Free plan first and upgrade only when BenchPilot works for you.

Team pilots

A team workspace starts with a 14-day pilot with no card. Nothing is charged during the pilot, so there is nothing to refund. If the team does not pay when the pilot ends, the workspace turns read-only for 30 days (export still works) and is then closed; no charge is ever made.

Exception: duplicate or failed activation

We make one narrow exception. If you were charged twice for the same purchase, or you were charged but your plan (Solo, a team plan, or an older Basic or Pro plan) was not activated on your account or team and support cannot activate it, contact us within 7 days of the payment with your account email and your Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). We will activate your plan or reverse the duplicate or failed charge to your original payment method (UPI account, card, bank account or wallet), which takes 5–7 working days.

Email nicklennox30@gmail.com to report it.

What a reversed payment changes

  • Your latest payment, fully reversed: your account (or team) goes back to the plan and end date it had before that payment. If that earlier end date has already passed, the plan ends.
  • An older payment, fully reversed: the days it added are taken off your current end date (converted by price if you have changed plan since).
  • Team plans: the same rules apply to the team's plan and paid-until date. If no paid time is left, the workspace follows the usual read-only period.
  • Partial refunds do not change the plan until together they add up to the full payment.
  • Founding offer: a fully reversed founding payment removes the time it bought, and no longer counts toward the first 100 founding places.
  • Referral reward: if a team's first payment earned someone 90 days of Solo through a "Bring BenchPilot to your team" link and that payment is fully reversed, the reward is taken back.

Cancellation

BenchPilot has no auto-renewal. Every payment is a one-time payment; we never charge you again on our own, so "cancelling" simply means not paying for another period. When a Solo plan ends you return to the Free plan and keep your saved data. When a team plan ends, the workspace turns read-only for 30 days, so the owner can export everything.
