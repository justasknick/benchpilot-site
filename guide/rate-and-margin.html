Rate and margin

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

Every submission has three numbers. Know them before you write the email.

  • The vendor's rate: what the vendor pays your company per hour (for example $70/hr C2C).
  • The consultant's pay: what your company pays the consultant per hour or per year.
  • Your margin: the difference, before your company's costs.

A simple example

The vendor offers $70/hr C2C. Your consultant is paid $58/hr. Your margin is $12/hr, about $2,000 a month at full time. Your company's costs (payroll taxes, insurance and benefits for a W2 or H-1B employee) come out of that margin, so the real profit is lower. Ask your manager what minimum margin your company needs.

Quoting a rate to a vendor

  • Quote the rate you want, not the lowest you would take. Vendors negotiate down.
  • If the post gives a range, aim for the top for a strong consultant.
  • Never change the rate after the client has seen it. It damages trust with the vendor.
