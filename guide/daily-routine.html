A day on the bench sales desk (US hours from India)

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

US recruiters post and reply during US business hours, so an Indian bench sales desk usually works the evening and night. New York is 9½ hours behind India in summer and 10½ in winter; Chicago is one hour further behind, California three.

A sample shift (Indian time)

  • 6:30 pm US East Coast morning starts. Check replies and follow-ups due today.
  • 7:00 pm Scroll LinkedIn for new requirements. Note the ones that fit your bench.
  • 8:00 pm Talk to the consultants who fit. Confirm interest, availability and rate.
  • 9:00 pm Send submissions, with an RTR where asked.
  • 11:00 pm Second round of posts as the US Central and West Coast mornings start.
  • 1:00 am Follow-ups and calls; update every submission's status.
  • 3:00 am Plan tomorrow: who is longest on the bench, which vendors to call.

Habits that pay

  • Push the consultants who have waited longest first.
  • Record every submission the moment you send it.
  • End each shift with an empty list of follow-ups due.
