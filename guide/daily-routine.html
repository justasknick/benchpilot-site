A day on the bench sales desk

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

US recruiters post and reply during US business hours, and the East Coast starts first. Plan your shift around their clock, whatever time zone your desk is in: the East Coast is one hour ahead of Central time and three hours ahead of the West Coast.

A sample shift (US Eastern time)

  • 9:00 am East Coast morning. Check replies and the follow-ups due today.
  • 9:30 am Scroll LinkedIn for new requirements. Note the ones that fit your bench.
  • 10:30 am Talk to the consultants who fit. Confirm interest, availability and rate.
  • 11:30 am Send submissions, with an RTR where asked.
  • 1:00 pm Second round of posts as the Central and West Coast mornings start.
  • 3:00 pm Follow-ups and calls; update every submission's status.
  • 5:00 pm Plan tomorrow: who is longest on the bench, which vendors to call.

Habits that pay

  • Push the consultants who have waited longest first.
  • Record every submission the moment you send it.
  • End each shift with an empty list of follow-ups due.
