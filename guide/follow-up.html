Following up without being a nuisance

2 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

Most submissions get no reply at first. A short, well-timed follow-up gets answers; daily chasing gets you ignored.

A simple rhythm

  • 2–3 working days after the submission: one short follow-up. "Any update on Ravi for the Java role at Lonestar Bank?"
  • One week later: a second follow-up, or a call if you have the number.
  • After that, move on, and keep the vendor for future roles.

Reading silence

No reply usually means the client hasn't decided, or the role was filled. It rarely means your consultant was rejected for something you can fix. Keep pitching other roles in the meantime.

Keep a record

Write down when you followed up and what you heard. Vendors that reply are worth more of your time next month.
