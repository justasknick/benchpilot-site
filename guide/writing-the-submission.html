Writing the submission email

4 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

A vendor reads dozens of submissions a day. Make yours the easy one to forward.

Subject line

Consultant name · Role · Location · Visa · Rate. For example: Ravi Kumar · Sr. Java Developer · Dallas, TX · H-1B · $68/hr C2C. Add the job ID if the post has one.

The pitch: four lines

  1. Who: name, title, years of experience.
  2. Fit: the 3–4 must-have skills from the post that the consultant has, in the post's own words.
  3. Logistics: current location, relocation if relevant, visa, availability (start date, interview slots).
  4. Rate and RTR: the C2C rate, and that the RTR is attached or ready.

The résumé

  • PDF or Word, named First_Last_Role.pdf.
  • The consultant's real experience. Never invent projects, employers or skills, and never change dates. It is dishonest and it ends careers when a background check finds it.
  • Highlight the matching skills near the top, honestly.

Never write

  • Personal ID numbers (SSN, passport) in a first email.
  • "Please find attached" with nothing else. Write the four lines.
  • A pitch for a consultant who doesn't fit the must-haves.
