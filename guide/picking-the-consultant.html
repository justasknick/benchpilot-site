Picking the right consultant

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

Sending the wrong consultant costs you more than sending none: vendors remember who wastes their time. Check four things, in this order.

  1. Must-have skills and years. If a must-have is missing, stop. Nice-to-haves only help you choose between two good fits.
  2. Location. Can the consultant work where the post says, today? For "locals only", the answer must be yes now, not after relocation.
  3. Visa. Is the consultant's work authorisation on the post's list? If the post says "no H-1B", an H-1B consultant is a no.
  4. Rate. Does the vendor's rate leave room for the consultant's pay and your margin? (See Rate and margin.)

When two consultants fit

Pick the one who has been on the bench longer, unless the other is clearly stronger. A consultant waiting 30 days or more needs a push today.

Ask the consultant first

Confirm interest, availability and the rate before you submit. Never submit someone who hasn't agreed to this specific role.
