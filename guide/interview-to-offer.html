The bench sales playbook · 8 of 12

From interview to offer

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

The usual stages

  1. Vendor screening: a short call to check the basics (skills, visa, rate, availability).
  2. Client rounds: usually one technical and one manager interview, sometimes a coding test.
  3. Offer: the vendor confirms the rate, start date and duration.
  4. Onboarding: background check, employment eligibility checks, the client's paperwork.

Preparing the consultant

  • Send the job description and the client's name before each round.
  • Make sure they can explain every project on their résumé in their own words.
  • Test the video link and the time zone for every interview. A US morning is an Indian evening.

Rate at the offer

The rate in the RTR is normally the rate at the offer. If the scope changed a lot during the interviews, raise it with the vendor before the offer, not after.
