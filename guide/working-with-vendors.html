Working with vendors

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

Your vendor list is the most valuable thing you build in this job. Treat it like one.

Know the tiers

  • Direct client: you deal with the end client itself. Rare and valuable.
  • Prime vendor: holds the client contract. Fast answers, better rates.
  • Implementation partner: runs the project for the client.
  • Sub-vendor: gets the role from another vendor. More layers, lower rates.

Trusted, neutral, avoid

Note which vendors reply, pay on time and treat consultants well. Stop pitching the ones that don't. One honest note ("slow to pay, no replies") saves you weeks.

Growing the list

  • Save every recruiter you work with, with email, phone and LinkedIn.
  • Reply quickly and politely, even with a "no fit right now".
  • Send your hotlist only to vendors who asked for it.
