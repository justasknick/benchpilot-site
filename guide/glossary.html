Bench sales glossary

4 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

Bench
Consultants employed by your company who are between projects and ready to start.
Bench sales recruiter
The person who markets those consultants to vendors and clients.
Requirement
An open role a vendor or client needs to fill.
Hotlist
A list of available consultants that a bench sales company shares. Not a requirement.
End client
The company where the consultant will work.
Prime vendor
The staffing firm with the direct contract with the end client.
Implementation partner
A services firm running the project for the end client.
MSP / VMS
Managed service provider / vendor management system: how large clients manage their staffing suppliers.
C2C
Corp-to-corp: your company invoices the vendor; the consultant stays your employee.
W2
The consultant is on the paying company's payroll.
1099
The consultant is an independent contractor.
Bill rate / pay rate
What the client pays per hour / what the consultant is paid.
RTR
Right to represent: permission for one vendor to submit a consultant to one client and role.
Submission
Sending a consultant's résumé to a vendor for a requirement.
Double submission
The same consultant reaching the same client through two firms. Usually means rejection.
USC / GC
US citizen / green card holder (permanent resident).
EAD
Employment authorisation document: GC-EAD, H4-EAD, L2-EAD and others.
H-1B
A work visa tied to a sponsoring employer; a new employer needs a transfer.
OPT / CPT
Work permission linked to a student visa.
Locals only
The consultant must already live near the work location.
Contract to hire
A contract the client may turn into a full-time job.
DOE
Depends on experience: the rate is open to discussion.
