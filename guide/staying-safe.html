Staying safe: etiquette, email law and personal data

3 min read · Updated 23 October 2026

On LinkedIn

  • Reply to the post's recruiter by email, as the post asks. Don't spam comments or DMs.
  • Use your real name and company. Fake profiles get banned, and vendors stop trusting your company.
  • Follow LinkedIn's own rules; your account is your livelihood.

Email to US companies

US commercial email is covered by the CAN-SPAM Act. In short: an honest subject line, your real name and company, a way to say "stop", and stop when asked. A one-to-one submission to a recruiter who asked for résumés is normal business email; mass mailing your hotlist to strangers is not.

Consultants' personal data

  • Share a résumé only with the consultant's consent, and only for roles they agreed to.
  • Never put SSNs, passport numbers or dates of birth in a first email. Send them only through the vendor's secure process, when the offer stage asks.
  • Delete old copies you don't need.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Your company's compliance or legal adviser has the final word.
